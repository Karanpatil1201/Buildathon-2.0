# SOP-STL-001: Surface Crazing & Thermal Microfissure Control
**Document ID**: DOC-SOP-001
**Standard**: ASTM A568 / EN 10163-2 Class A
**Applicability**: Hot Strip Mill & Continuous Rolling Lines

### Description & Root Causes
Surface crazing consists of an irregular network of fine microfissures across the hot-rolled strip surface. 
Primary root causes include:
1. Excessive thermal-mechanical stress during high-reduction rolling passes.
2. Uneven water spray quenching across the runout cooling table.
3. Overheated entry temperature of steel slabs exceeding 1150°C.

### Remediation & CAPA Requirements
1. Inspect work roll cooling water headers for clogged or misaligned nozzles.
2. Verify slab reheating furnace exit temperatures do not exceed metallurgical limit.
3. Moderate inter-stand rolling tension and reduce strip speed by 8-12% until surface grain texture stabilizes.
4. Quarantined coils exhibiting crazing depth > 0.08 mm must be rejected or skin-passed under concession review.
