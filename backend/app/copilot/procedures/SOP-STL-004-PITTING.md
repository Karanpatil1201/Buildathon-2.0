# SOP-STL-004: Pitted Surface & Acid Pickling Erosion Rectification
**Document ID**: DOC-SOP-004
**Standard**: ASTM A568 / ISO 16160
**Applicability**: Continuous Pickling Lines & Cold Reduction Mills

### Description & Root Causes
Pitted surface manifests as small, localized depressions or crater clusters across the steel sheet.
Primary root causes include:
1. Over-pickling caused by line stoppage or excessive hydrochloric acid concentration.
2. Insufficient corrosion inhibitor concentration in pickling baths #2 and #3.
3. Indented roll debris from previous coil spalls.

### Remediation & CAPA Requirements
1. Sample and titrate hydrochloric acid concentration (maintain between 8% - 12% at 75°C - 85°C).
2. Measure pickling inhibitor dosing rate (standard: 0.15% - 0.25% v/v).
3. Inspect and wipe backup rolls for embedded particulate debris.
4. Deep pitting exceeding 5% of nominal sheet thickness requires scrap disposition.
