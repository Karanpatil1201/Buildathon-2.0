# SOP-STL-006: Coil Handling, Scratches & Roller Mark Elimination
**Document ID**: DOC-SOP-006
**Standard**: EN 10130 / ASTM A1008
**Applicability**: Skin-Pass Mills, Runout Tables & Downcoilers

### Description & Root Causes
Scratches are longitudinal mechanical grooves, roll marks, or abrasions caused by contact with stationary equipment or roll slippage.
Primary root causes include:
1. Worn or unlubricated runout table conveyor rollers.
2. Accumulation of metal fines on coil wrapper aprons.
3. Coil slippage on the coiler mandrel during initial tension buildup.

### Remediation & CAPA Requirements
1. Inspect and dress runout table rollers; replace rollers with flat spots or scoring.
2. Clean and vacuum metal debris from downcoiler wrapper plates each shift.
3. Verify coiler belt wrapper tension and apply protective polyurethane padding.
4. Light scratches < 0.02 mm depth may be polished out if customer dimensional tolerance permits.
