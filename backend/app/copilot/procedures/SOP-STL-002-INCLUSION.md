# SOP-STL-002: Non-Metallic Oxide & Slag Inclusion Mitigation
**Document ID**: DOC-SOP-002
**Standard**: ASTM E45 Method A (Inclusion Rating)
**Applicability**: Hot-Rolled Coils & Cold-Rolled Strip Lines

### Description & Root Causes
Inclusions are non-metallic foreign particles (primarily alumina Al2O3, silicates, and deoxidation slag) pressed into the steel surface.
Primary root causes include:
1. Air ingress through worn ladle shroud seals during teeming.
2. Incomplete slag separation in the tundish or vortex formation during ladle changeover.
3. Descaling header water pressure drop below standard operating threshold (180 bar).

### Remediation & CAPA Requirements
1. Immediately verify Argon gas shroud seal on the ladle stream to prevent atmospheric re-oxidation.
2. Inspect high-pressure descaler nozzle pressure and clear obstructed tips.
3. Check tundish liquid level to prevent vortex slag pull-down.
4. Any batch with > 2 confirmed critical inclusions must be placed on immediate Quality Hold (`ON_HOLD`).
