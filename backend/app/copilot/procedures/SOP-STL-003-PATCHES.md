# SOP-STL-003: Surface Patches, Scabs & Friction Flaw Containment
**Document ID**: DOC-SOP-003
**Standard**: EN 10163-2 Class B
**Applicability**: Heavy Steel Plates & Structural Carbon Strips

### Description & Root Causes
Patches (also termed scabs or rolled-in peel) are localized surface crusts or irregular scale patches adhering to the plate.
Primary root causes include:
1. Work roll surface spalling, fire-cracking, or roughness degradation.
2. Misaligned entry side guides rubbing the strip edge.
3. Inadequate high-pressure descaling before roughing stands.

### Remediation & CAPA Requirements
1. Conduct optical inspection of upper and lower work rolls on stands F1-F4 for localized spalling.
2. Schedule work roll replacement if surface roughness Ra exceeds 1.8 µm.
3. Re-align entry guide clearances to prevent lateral contact gouges.
4. Rework through precision surface grinding is permitted if remaining thickness meets ASTM minimum tolerance.
