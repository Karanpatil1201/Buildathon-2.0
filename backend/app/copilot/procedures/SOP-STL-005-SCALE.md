# SOP-STL-005: Rolled-in Mill Scale Prevention & Containment
**Document ID**: DOC-SOP-005
**Standard**: ASTM A568 / EN 10163-1
**Applicability**: Hot Strip Roughing & Finishing Stands

### Description & Root Causes
Rolled-in scale occurs when primary or secondary iron oxides (magnetite Fe3O4 and hematite Fe2O3) are not removed by descalers and are crushed into the steel substrate by the work rolls.
Primary root causes include:
1. Descaling pump pressure loss below 175 bar.
2. Worn or eroded tungsten carbide descaling nozzles producing uneven spray fans.
3. Excess oxygen (> 1.5%) in the slab reheat furnace soaking zone.

### Remediation & CAPA Requirements
1. Verify descaling header pressure gauge; minimum required dynamic pressure is 185 bar.
2. Replace descaling nozzles exceeding 200 operating hours or showing spray angle distortion > 5°.
3. Calibrate reheat furnace fuel-to-air ratio to maintain an inert/reducing atmosphere.
4. Scale-affected coils must be diverted to secondary pickling or re-rolled under concession.
